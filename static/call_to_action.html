This program is to provide confirmations of scheduled visits to the Mikvah of Washington Heights. We only send automated texts regarding your visit, and not for any other purpose. Generally we only send a single message, immediately after you schedule your appointment. If you reschedule your appointment, we will send a text for that rescheduling. In exceptional circumstances, we may send you a second text during the week of your visit, for example if the mikvah cannot open as planned.

If you opt in via the web site, by clicking the radio button "Send a confirmation text", you should receive a text message originating from (848) 266-6360.

The identity of the organization being represented by this text message is CONGREGATION KHAL ADATH JESHURUN INC, which is owner and operator of the Mikvah of Washington Heights.

As always, message and data rates may apply for any messages sent to you from us and to us from you. You should receive a message no more than twice per scheduling. If you have any questions about your text plan or data plan, it is best to contact your wireless provider. No other fees or charges will be applied.
